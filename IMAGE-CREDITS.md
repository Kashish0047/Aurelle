# Image Credits

Record the source and licence of every image on the site. Keep this file up to date for each
client. It protects you and the client if anyone asks where a photo came from.

## Template placeholders

All images in `public/images/` that show a soft colour gradient with an italic label are
**generated placeholders** made by `scripts/generate-placeholders.mjs`. They contain no third-party
content and may be used freely, but they are meant to be replaced before launch.

Accepted sources:
- **The salon's own photos.** Confirm the client owns them or has the photographer's permission.
- **Unsplash** (unsplash.com/license) or **Pexels** (pexels.com/license). Free for commercial use;
  attribution appreciated, not required.

Never use images from Pinterest, Google Images, other salons' websites or social media without
written permission.
