# Your Website Admin — User Guide

This guide is for the salon owner and staff. Everything here is done in the browser; you never
need to touch code.

**Open the admin:** go to `yourwebsite.com/admin` and sign in with the email and password you
were given. For safety you're signed out after an hour without activity. Just sign in again.

> Changes appear on your website within a few seconds of pressing **Save**. If you don't see them,
> refresh the website page.

---

## The dashboard

The first page shows:
- **Services**, **Active offers**, **Bookings this week**
- **Waiting for you**: new booking requests you haven't answered yet. It's highlighted when there are some.
- **Latest bookings**, with a WhatsApp button for each
- **+ Add offer** and **+ Add photo** shortcuts

On a phone, tap **☰** (top left) to open the menu.

## Things that work the same everywhere

- **Add:** the button at the top right of each page (e.g. *+ Add service*).
- **Edit:** tap **Edit** on any row, change what you need, press **Save**.
- **Show / hide:** the switch on each row. Hidden items stay saved but disappear from the website.
  This is often better than deleting.
- **Change the order:** drag the **⠿** handle. On the keyboard, focus the handle and use ↑ / ↓.
  The website shows items in the same order.
- **Delete:** asks you to confirm first and offers *Hide instead*. Deleting also removes its photos.
- **Search and filters** sit above each list.
- **Photos:** tap *Upload photo*. Any normal photo works (JPG, PNG, WebP, under 5 MB). It's resized
  for you, so phone photos are fine.

## Services

Your price list, grouped into categories (Hair, Skin, Nails…).

- **Name, description, price, duration.** Turn on **"from" price** for prices that vary
  (shows *from ₹7,500*).
- **Popular** adds a small badge to the card.
- Services appear in the website's **booking form** automatically, with their price and duration.
- **Categories** (button at the top of Services) are the filter chips on the website. A category
  can only be deleted once no service uses it.

## Packages

Bridal or spa bundles shown as price cards. Add the features one per line. Turn on **Featured** for
the one you want to stand out (the dark middle card). Only one package can be featured.

## Offers & Banners

A limited-time deal shown as a banner with a countdown.

- **Small label** (e.g. *25% OFF*), **title**, **description**, optional **promo code**.
- **Starts / Ends** are in the salon's time. The banner **appears and disappears by itself** on
  those dates.
- **Active** off pauses an offer without deleting it.
- If several offers are live at once, the website shows the **first one in your list**. Drag to choose.
- Status badges: *Live now*, *Scheduled* (starts later), *Ended*, *Off*.

## Recent Work (photo gallery)

- **Add many photos at once:** drag them onto the box, or tap *Choose photos* (up to 20).
- Then tap **Edit** on a photo to add a caption, category or tile shape (square / tall / wide).
- **Before / after:** add a *"Before" photo* to any picture (or use *+ Before / after pair*).
  The website then shows a slider visitors can drag.
- Always fill in **Photo description** (e.g. *"Bride with soft glam makeup"*). It helps Google and
  visitors who use screen readers.

## Testimonials

Copy your best Google or Instagram reviews here: what they said, their name, the stars and,
optionally, the service. Only add a client's photo with their permission.

## Team

Name, role, speciality, a portrait photo and (optional) their Instagram link (the full
`https://instagram.com/…` address).

## FAQ

Questions and answers shown on the website (and to Google). Keep answers short and friendly.

## Bookings

Every booking made on your website. (This page fills up only when online booking is connected to
the admin. Your web developer sets this up once.)

- **Filters:** *Upcoming*, *Today*, *This week*, *Past*, *All*, or pick dates, a status, or search
  by name, phone or reference.
- **Status:** change it with the dropdown on each booking:
  - **Waiting for you**: a new request. Confirm it with the client, then set **Confirmed**.
  - **Completed** / **No-show**: after the appointment.
  - **Cancelled**: frees the time slot for other clients.
- **WhatsApp** opens a chat with the client with a ready message (confirmation, cancellation or
  thank-you). Check it, then press send.
- **Download CSV** gives you a spreadsheet of the bookings currently shown (opens in Excel or
  Google Sheets).
- A cancelled booking can only be re-opened if that time still has a free seat.

## Settings

- **Business details:** name, phone, WhatsApp number (country code + number, e.g. `919876543210`),
  email, address, Google Maps links, Instagram / Facebook / YouTube.
- **Opening hours:** turn each day on or off and set the times. Add daily **breaks** (e.g. lunch).
  Online booking only offers times inside these hours.
- **Closed days & holidays:** add dates when you're closed. Nobody can book them.
- **Theme & logo:** choose one of four colour themes (the preview shows how it looks) and upload
  a logo (a wide logo with a transparent background looks best).
- **Hero:** the big text at the top of your home page.
- **Sections:** switch whole parts of the home page off (their menu links disappear too).
- **How you look on Google:** the title and description shown in Google results, with a preview.

## Good habits

- Prefer **hiding** over deleting; you can always switch it back on.
- Keep photo descriptions filled in.
- Check **Waiting for you** on the dashboard every day.
- Don't share your password; ask your developer to add a separate login for each staff member.

## Something's not right?

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "That email and password don't match" | Check for typos. After 5 tries, wait 15 minutes. Still stuck? Ask your developer to reset it. |
| I was signed out | Normal after an hour of no activity. Sign in again. |
| My change isn't on the website | Refresh the website page. Check the item's switch is **on**, and for offers, that today is between the start and end dates. |
| A section disappeared | Sections with nothing to show (e.g. no published team members) hide themselves. Also check **Settings → Sections**. |
| Photo won't upload | Use a JPG/PNG/WebP under 5 MB and check your internet connection. |
| "This item is still in use" | You're deleting a category that still has services. Move or delete those services first. |
| "That time is fully booked now" | Someone else took that slot. Offer the client another time. |
